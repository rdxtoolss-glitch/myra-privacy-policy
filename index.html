1. Summary

  • MYRA runs mostly on your phone. We do not run a server that receives your voice, chats, contacts, messages, screen content or files.
  • MYRA uses your own Google Gemini API key. Your voice, text, images and documents go directly from your phone to Google under that key. We never see them or your key.
  • The only data we receive is licensing data (device ID, phone model, Android version, license key). See Section 4.
  • We have no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs and no tracking. We do not sell or share your data.
  • MYRA needs many sensitive permissions because it controls your phone by voice. Each one is used only for the feature that needs it (Section 6).

2. Who this applies to

This policy covers the MYRA Android app and the update and licensing services it contacts. It does not cover Google, GitHub, or other third-party services, which have their own policies (Section 5).

4. Data sent to us (Mobo Devs licensing server)

MYRA has a free trial and paid license keys. To manage this, the app contacts our licensing server (hosted on Supabase).

What is sent:

  • Device ID. An app-generated ID like DEV-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. It is a one-way SHA-256 hash of your phone manufacturer, model and Android ID. It cannot be reversed to your Android ID.
  • Device model (for example, "Samsung SM-S918B").
  • Android version and API level.
  • License key you enter, with its activation time, expiry and status.
  • Last-seen time (a timestamp each time the app checks in).
  • Your IP address, as with any internet request, which our hosting provider can see in its technical logs.

Why: to activate and verify licenses, bind a key to one device, restore your license after reinstall, and block revoked or abused keys.

What is not sent: your name, email, phone number, contacts, messages, voice, chats, location, files or Gemini key.

Retention: device and license records are kept while the license is valid or the device is in use, and as needed to prevent fraud and handle support. To request deletion, contact us (Section 10).

5. Third-party services

5.1 Google Gemini (core feature)

When you talk or type, MYRA connects to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) using your API key. Depending on what you do, this may include:

  • your voice audio (streamed while a voice session is active) and text;
  • images from the camera, gallery, Scanner or Study workspace;
  • documents you import (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, code);
  • screen frames or screenshots, when you use screen-reading features;
  • results of tools you invoke by voice, such as contact names, calendar entries, recent notifications, SMS, call-log details, device status or weather.

Google processes this under your agreement with Google and the Gemini API Additional Terms and Google Privacy Policy. Whether Google uses your data to improve its products depends on your API key's billing tier and settings, which you control with Google. We do not receive or store this data.

5.2 Update service (GitHub)

MYRA checks raw.githubusercontent.com/rdxtoolss-glitch/Myra-updates for a small version.json file and downloads new APKs from our GitHub releases. GitHub sees your IP address and request. The app checks the file size and SHA-256 hash and only accepts approved hosts before offering an install. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5.3 Weather (Open-Meteo)

For weather, MYRA sends a city name (for geocoding) or approximate coordinates from your device's last-known location to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). Location is requested only for this feature and is not stored or sent to us.

5.4 Market data (Markets workspace)

The Markets workspace fetches public prices from CoinGecko, Binance, Frankfurter and Open ER API. These requests contain no personal data beyond your IP address.

5.5 Other apps and links

When you ask MYRA to open WhatsApp, YouTube, Spotify, Google Maps, the dialer or the browser, MYRA hands the request to that app. Your data is then handled under that app's policy. MYRA only checks whether certain apps (WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Spotify, YouTube, Google Maps) are installed so it can launch them.

7. Accessibility service disclosure

MYRA uses Android's Accessibility Service to let you control your phone by voice. When you enable it, MYRA can:

  • read on-screen text and UI elements to understand what is open;
  • tap, swipe, type and scroll for you;
  • take screenshots.

MYRA uses this only to carry out your voice or text commands (for example "open Settings and turn on Wi-Fi", "read my screen", "reply to this message"). On-screen content may be sent to Google Gemini when you ask MYRA to look at or act on your screen (Section 5.1). MYRA does not use the service for advertising, profiling or selling data. MYRA does not collect or store passwords you type. You can turn the service off at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

8. Security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Cleartext traffic is disabled.
  • The Gemini API key and license signature are encrypted with a hardware-backed Android Keystore key.
  • App data is excluded from Android backups.
  • Optional app lock (PIN, pattern, biometric, voice passphrase), per-app lock, and Private Mode (no chat history saved).
  • The release app is signed, and updates are verified by SHA-256 before installation.
  • Debug logging is turned off in release builds.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your phone locked, protect your Gemini API key and do not share it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete local data: clear chats, memories, notes and workspaces in the app, or use Android Settings → Apps → MYRA → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall.
  • Revoke access: turn off any permission, Accessibility, Notification access or Device administrator in Android Settings.
  • Private Mode: stops saving chat history.
  • Gemini data: manage or delete it in your Google account or API project, and revoke your key in Google AI Studio.
  • Licensing data: email us to request access to or deletion of the device and license records linked to your Device ID (shown on the license screen). Deleting a record may deactivate your license.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or object. Contact us to use them. We do not sell personal information.

10. Children

MYRA is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Google's Gemini API also has its own age requirements. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our licensing server and the third parties in Section 5 may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. By using MYRA you understand your data may be transferred to those countries.
